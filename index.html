How it plays

Part word game, part block puzzle. Every move is a little of both.

01

Place your pieces

You get three lettered pieces at a time. Tap or drag them onto the 9×9 board.

02

Spell words

Line up three or more letters across or down to spell a word, and it clears. Longer words and rare letters score more.

03

Clear for combos

Fill a row, column or 3×3 box to clear it too. Clear a word and a line at once for a SPELLBLOCK!

Three ways to play

Each mode keeps its own game, so you can switch anytime and pick up where you left off.

Endless

Chase a high score

Play as long as you can. Level up as you go, and the pieces get trickier the higher you climb.

Daily

Same board for everyone

A fresh puzzle each day that every player shares. Build a streak and compare results with friends.

Zen

No pressure

Mostly blank blocks and a calmer pace, for when you just want to unwind.

Play with friends

Spellblocks uses Game Center, so there's no account to make.

Two leaderboards

Rank by Highest Score (your best game) and Total Score (everything you've ever earned).

Friends tab

See just you and your Game Center friends, and find out who's really the Spellblocks master.

Share your score

Send your score, best word and streak to the group chat anytime.
